# Draw a catalytic cycle

Put a catalytic cycle on the page, write the catalyst and two intermediates on its line, and add the arrows for what comes in and what goes out.

1. {#open-quick-add} Double-click an empty spot on the page. A row of icons opens.
2. {#scheme} Choose *Scheme*, the icon with a plus and a curved arrow. A second row opens below it.
3. {#cycle} Choose *Catalytic cycle*, the circle of arrows. A circle is put down where you double-clicked.
4. {#catalyst-open} Double-click the top of the circle's line. The row of icons opens there.
5. {#catalyst-text} Choose *Text*, the T.
6. {#catalyst} Type `L2Pd(0)` and press Enter. The 2 is set lower by itself, and the circle opens round the words.
7. {#second-open} Double-click the circle's line at the lower right.
8. {#second-text} Choose *Text*.
9. {#second} Type `L2Pd(Ar)X` and press Enter. An arrow now runs clockwise from the first words to these.
10. {#third-open} Double-click the circle's line at the lower left.
11. {#third-text} Choose *Text*.
12. {#third} Type `L2Pd(Ar)Ar'` and press Enter. The circle is now three arrows, clockwise.
13. {#arrow-in-menu} Right-click the line at the upper right, between the first two words.
14. {#arrow-in} Choose *Arrow coming in*. An arrow curves in from outside and joins the line there.
15. {#arrow-in-words} Type `Ar-X` at once, and press Enter. The words go at the arrow's far end.
16. {#metal-in-menu} Right-click the line at the bottom, a little right of the middle, between the second and third words.
17. {#metal-in} Choose *Arrow coming in*.
18. {#metal-in-words} Type `Ar'-M` at once, and press Enter.
19. {#metal-out-menu} Right-click the same part of the line, a little left of the middle.
20. {#metal-out} Choose *Arrow going out*. On this part of the line, one arrow now comes in and one goes out.
21. {#metal-out-words} Type `M-X` at once, and press Enter.
22. {#arrow-out-menu} Right-click the line at the upper left, between the last words and the first.
23. {#arrow-out} Choose *Arrow going out*. An arrow leaves the line there and curves out.
24. {#arrow-out-words} Type `Ar-Ar'` at once, and press Enter.
25. {#let-go} Click an empty spot on the page. The cycle is let go, and you see it as it is drawn: what comes in and what goes out at each stage, all running clockwise.
