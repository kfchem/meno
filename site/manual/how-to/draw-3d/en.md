# Draw a structure and see it in 3D

Draw a ring with the pointer, and its 3D structure rises out of the drawing.

1. {#open-quick-add} Double-click an empty spot on the page.
2. {#chain} Choose *Chain*.
3. {#lead-ring} Move the pointer round the six corners of a ring, without pressing the button, and click where you began.
4. {#right-click} Right-click an atom of the ring.
5. {#make-3d} Choose *3D structure*.
6. {#allow} The first time, Meno asks before it downloads RDKit: choose *Allow and download*.
