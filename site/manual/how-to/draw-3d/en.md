# Draw a structure and see it in 3D

Draw a ring with the pointer, and its 3D structure rises out of the drawing.

1. {#set-up} The first time Meno starts, a card at the bottom left asks *Set up chemistry?* Choose *Set up*: Meno gets its chemistry plugin from the list of plugins online and sets it up, once. If no card shows, chemistry is already set up: go on to step 2.
2. {#open-quick-add} Double-click an empty spot on the page. A row of icons opens.
3. {#chain} Choose *Chain*, the zigzag icon.
4. {#lead-ring} Move the pointer round the six corners of a ring, without pressing the button, and click where you began.
5. {#right-click} Right-click an atom of the ring.
6. {#make-3d} Choose *3D structure*: the icon marked *3D* in the top row of the menu. The 3D structure rises out of the drawing and comes to rest beside it.
7. {#let-go} Click an empty spot on the page. The drawing and its 3D structure stand side by side.
