# Take structures out of a paper and make slides of them

Read a paper beside your work, take two of its structures out onto the page as structures you can edit, give each a slide, and present them.

1. {#open} Press ⌘O (Ctrl+O on Windows), and choose the paper's PDF in the window that opens. It is put on the page.
2. {#read} Double-click the PDF. It opens in a column on the right of the page.
3. {#take-first} In the column, point at a structure: it lights up. Drag it onto the page.
4. {#take-second} Drag the next structure onto the page too.
5. {#choose-first} Press and hold the first structure to choose the whole of it.
6. {#menu-first} Right-click it.
7. {#slide-first} Choose *Slide around these*. A slide is drawn round the structure.
8. {#choose-second} Press and hold the second structure.
9. {#menu-second} Right-click it.
10. {#slide-second} Choose *Slide around these*.
11. {#present-menu} Right-click empty space on the page.
12. {#present} Choose *Present*. The window fills the screen with the first slide. Press Escape to end the talk.
