# Settings

Where Meno's settings are, and what each part of them holds. They are Meno's on this computer, and a change is kept as you make it.

## Opening Settings {#opening}

*Settings*, the button at the top right of the window, opens them in a tab of their own, which closes like any tab. Along their top are their parts: *General*, *Drawing*, *3D*, *Chemistry*, *Files*, *Calculations*, *Plugins*, *Dictionary* and *Network*.

## A workspace's own styles {#workspace}

The drawing style and the 3D styles in Settings are the ones new workspaces start with. A workspace keeps the styles it was made in, in its own file. To change them, right-click empty space on its page and choose *Styles…*; *Use Settings' styles* there takes Settings' again. When Settings' style changes while a workspace is open, Meno asks (*Settings has a new style*): *Keep its style*, or *Apply to this workspace*.

## General {#general}

- *The wheel turned upwards*: *Zooms in* or *Zooms out*.
- *Drawing resolution*: how finely the page is drawn - *2×*, *Matching the screen* or *1×*.
- *Read labels as they are meant* and *Write words on the page as they are meant*: labels and words typed in shorthand become what they mean.
- *Colours*: the palette - *Meno*, *Okabe–Ito*, or a set of your own, made with *New set*.
- *Say a workspace is large past*: the size at which Meno asks about a large workspace as it saves it.
- *Meno's log*, with *Open log folder*, and *Licences*.

## Drawing {#drawing}

*Drawing style*: the style new workspaces are drawn in - *Meno*, *ACS 1996*, *RSC*, *Wiley* or *Nature*, or one of your own made from them with *Save as style…*. Every part of it, from *Bond length* and *Line thickness* to labels and arrows, can be changed; *Find a setting* finds one, and each change can be reset. *Preview* shows the style as it is.

## 3D {#3d}

*Molecules in 3D*: the styles new workspaces show molecules in, as *Primary* and *Secondary*; how each style draws a molecule, from *Atoms* to *Hydrogens on carbon*; *Atom colours*; and, under *Every style*, the light, orbitals and densities, ribbons and turning.

## Chemistry {#chemistry}

- *Point out atoms with too many bonds*: a mark, on the page only, on an atom drawn with more bonds than it takes.
- Which plugin does each thing for Meno - from *SMILES to a structure, and back* to *Pictures and films of molecules in 3D, rendered* - and, where two plugins can, which of them; its options stand under it.
- *Keep conformers within*: conformers far above the lowest are set aside as they are made.

## Files {#files}

- A table of the kinds of file Meno opens, with what reads and writes each.
- *Opening files*: the formats Meno can be the app for, and *Use Meno*.
- *Copied pictures*: *Resolution*.

## Calculations {#calculations}

- The options each kind of calculation step starts with, by what runs it: each plugin added, and Meno. A step put on the page starts with these; options changed in a step are that step's alone.
- *Jobs*: *Cores for each job*, *Cores jobs may use*, *Memory jobs may use*; *Where jobs run* - *Choose…* a folder, or *Meno's own*; and *Finished jobs' files*, with *Clear*.
- *Procedures*: the ones you saved from the page with *Save as procedure…*, with *Rename*, *Save as file…* and *Remove*.

## Plugins {#plugins}

Every plugin Meno offers, with *Add* and *Remove*: the manual's Plugins page says more.

## Dictionary {#dictionary}

What each label stands for - Boc, DMP, XPhos, Grubbs II - drawn.

- *Yours*: labels of your own. *Add a label* asks for the label, how else it is written, its name, and its structure as SMILES, with `*` where it is attached. Or select a group on the page and choose *Save as abbreviation…* on its menu.
- Then Meno's own: *Groups*, *Reagents*, *Ligands*, *Buchwald precatalysts* and *Counter-anions*.
- *Find a label, name or SMILES* looks through them all.

## Network {#network}

- *Work offline*.
- *Keep Meno up to date*, with *Look now* ([Installing](installing#updates)).
- *Allowed*: what has been allowed to use the network, each with *Withdraw*.
- *Connections since Meno started*: every connection, when, for what, where to, and how much went each way.
