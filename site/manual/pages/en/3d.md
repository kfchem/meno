# 3D structures

How to make a structure's 3D shape from its drawing, show it in the style you want, turn it and measure it, and look through its conformers.

## Making one from a drawing {#making}

Right-click an atom or a bond of a drawing and choose *3D structure*. For several structures at once, choose them, right-click, and choose *3D structures*. The 3D structure rises out of the drawing and comes to rest beside it, where it is in view; several made together stand in a row.

- It is made by the RDKit plugin. The first time, Meno asks before it downloads RDKit ([Installing](installing#plugins)). A note at the bottom right says how it is going, with *Stop*.
- Where the drawing leaves a stereocentre or a double bond's geometry open, Meno asks first (*Stereo not drawn*): *Make one* of the stereoisomers, all of them - the button says how many, as *Make all 4* - or *Cancel*.

## The drawing and its 3D structure {#linked}

The two stay tied to each other.

- Point at an atom in either, and the same atom lights up in the other.
- Change the drawing, and the 3D structure stays as it was and says so under it: "Its drawing has changed". *Make again* there, or *Make again from the drawing* on its right-click menu, makes it again from the drawing as it is now.
- *Turn like the drawing*, on its right-click menu, turns it to face you as the drawing does.
- A molecule in 3D with no drawing - one opened from a file - gets one with *Draw as formula*.

## Styles {#styles}

Each molecule in 3D has two styles, a primary and a secondary: at first, ball and stick and space-filling. Double-click a molecule to switch between them; the molecules chosen with it switch too. The first icon on its right-click menu, named after the other style, does the same.

- Right-click empty space and choose *Styles…* for the workspace's styles. Under *3D*, drag a style onto *Primary* or *Secondary*, or right-click a style and choose *Use as primary* or *Use as secondary*. The light is set there too: *Light from all round*, *Light from above right*, *Shadows*.
- How each style draws a molecule - its atoms, ball size, bond thickness, colours - is set in Settings, *3D*.

## Hydrogens {#hydrogens}

Hydrogens on carbon can be hidden, in Settings, *3D*: *Hydrogens on carbon*, *Hidden*. A molecule without them is shown wrongly, so Meno asks first: tick the box to say you understand, then choose *Hide hydrogens anyway*, or *Keep hydrogens*. While they are hidden, the molecule is marked *C–H hidden*.

## Turning and moving {#turning}

- Drag a molecule to turn it about its centre. Let go while it is turning, and it keeps turning.
- Press and hold a molecule to choose it, then drag to move it with whatever else is chosen. Hold ⌘ (Ctrl on Windows) and click to add a molecule or take it out.
- A right or middle drag moves the page; the wheel or a pinch zooms the page.
- *Turn once*, on its right-click menu, turns it round once and stops where it began. *Reset orientation* turns it back as it came.
- *Magnification* shows it larger or smaller, from 50 % to 300 %; *Back to 100 %* returns it to its true size.

## Measuring {#measuring}

Click two atoms of a molecule in 3D, three or four, then right-click: *Measure distance*, *Measure angle* or *Measure torsion angle* puts the value on it. Where the atoms can move, *Set distance…*, *Set angle…* or *Set torsion angle…* changes it, or double-click a value shown and type another; Enter sets it, and Escape leaves it as it was. *Delete measurement*, on the value's right-click menu, takes it away.

## Conformers {#conformers}

*3D structure* makes the molecule's conformers, not one geometry alone. They stand in one place, one shown at a time, and a chip under the molecule says which: its number of how many, its energy above the lowest, and its share of the set.

- Click the chip to open it: a bar for each conformer, its energy above the lowest. Point at a bar for its energy; press it to show that conformer, or drag along the bars to go through them. *How it was made* says how they were made, and how many were set aside.
- *Show all conformers*, on the molecule's right-click menu, draws the others over the one shown, each as dark as it is likely; *Show one conformer* goes back.
- Conformers far above the lowest are set aside as they are made: Settings, *Chemistry*, *Keep conformers within* (30 kcal/mol at first).
