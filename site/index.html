A workspace for chemists

Draw it, see it in 3D, calculate it. On one page.

A structure you draw rises into three dimensions, opens into its conformers and runs through calculations, with the paper you are reading beside it. Meno keeps them together, so the next idea starts where the last result ended.

1 · Draw

Draw the way you think.

Lead a chain across the honeycomb, round a ring and on, and type the label you mean. Step back, and the page holds the molecules you work with.

ACS 1996 proportions bond 14.4 pt · line 0.6 pt

2 · In 3D

Raise it off the page.

The drawing rises into three dimensions and steps aside to stand beside it. Turn it under the pointer.

C47H51NO14 11 stereocentres

3 · Conformers

See every shape it takes.

It comes as a set of conformers, each with its energy. Run along the bars to step through them in place.

29 conformers from the drawing

4 · Calculate

Put the calculation on the page.

Pull a wire out of a structure and add steps to it: a conformer search, the conformer you choose, an optimisation. They run as jobs on your computer, and each result lands beside its step.

Conformers → Choose one → Optimise

5 · Read

Keep the paper beside the molecule.

Open a PDF on the page and read it in a column. Take a figure out onto the page; it remembers where it came from.

How it behaves

A research instrument you can trust.

Network

Nothing goes out unasked

Meno asks before anything reaches the internet, shows every connection as it is made, and works offline whenever you switch it off.

Plugins

Programs you choose

Chemistry tools come as plugins, set up the first time they are needed, after Meno asks. Calculation programs you have installed are used where they are.

.meno

One file for the work

A workspace keeps its drawings, models, results and PDFs together in one file you can save, send and open again.

Download

Install Meno.

The latest version. Both downloads come from the project's releases on GitHub.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Download the installer

Meno_<version>_x64-setup.exe

  1. Open the installer you downloaded.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. Windows says this because Meno's installer is not yet signed with a certificate it knows.
  3. The installer asks nothing. A small window shows while it installs Meno for your account alone, so it needs no administrator's password, and Meno opens when it is done.
  4. Afterwards, open Meno from the Start menu or the desktop.

Mac

Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13 or later

Download the disk image

Meno_<version>_aarch64.dmg

  1. Open the disk image and drag Meno into Applications.
  2. Open Meno from Applications. The first time, macOS says it could not verify that Meno is free of malware. Choose Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Under Security, choose Open Anyway beside the line about Meno, and give your password when asked.
  4. Open Meno again. If macOS asks once more, choose Open Anyway. It remembers this, so you do it once.

macOS asks because Meno is not yet notarised by Apple. Open it from Applications, not from the disk image, so that it can update itself.

It keeps itself up to date

The first time it starts, Meno asks whether it may look for new versions. If you agree, it downloads them in the background and installs them when you quit, or at once with Restart now. You can change your mind in Settings, Network. To uninstall, or to find where Meno keeps its settings, see the README.
