# Correctness audit — October 2026

What Meno's outputs show against what its canvas shows. It was begun after atom numbers were found missing from every picture of a molecule in 3D (#500). Each finding is rated as in the [September audit](AUDIT-2026-09.md):

- **Severity**: user impact if left alone (High / Medium / Low).
- **Fix risk**: how likely a fix is to regress behaviour or the look (Low / Medium / High).

Status: **#n** = the PR that addresses it; **Ruled** = a rule the maintainer has changed, its fix to come; **Open** = not yet measured, or backlog.

The known answer throughout is **the canvas**: the same molecule, in the same look, with the same things shown. Each output was measured in the built app on a Mac, at Settings' 600 dpi for pictures:
- the copy: the PNG on the clipboard;
- Export… → SVG picture: the picture embedded in the SVG, and its text.

Render (the Blender plugin) is the films lane's, and is reported to them.

## Pixel thresholds on the picture path

A picture is drawn at its size in print, so a threshold written in the canvas's pixels means something else there. Every one on the 3D picture path was checked (`render3d.ts`, `occlusionMaterial.ts`, `jointNumbers.ts`, `style3d.ts`).

| ID | Threshold | Kind | Finding | Sev | Risk | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Atom numbers' least height, `NUMBER_LEAST_PX` (6) | **Hides** what is under it | Counted in the canvas's pixels (`× cssPx`) on a picture drawn at print size: at 600 dpi some 37 of the picture's pixels, so a digit had to stand about 1.6 mm tall to be drawn, and almost none was. The docs said it was counted in the picture's own pixels. | High | Low | #500 |
| P2 | Lines on balls' least width, `LINE_LEAST_PX × cssPx` | **Floor**: `max(width, least)` | Keeps a line at least 0.5 of the screen's pixels wide, some 0.13 mm in print. It never hides anything, so `× cssPx` is meant. | — | — | Fine |
| P3 | Outlines' least width, `lineWidth(…, cssPx / pxPerWorld)` | **Floor**, as P2 | As P2. | — | — | Fine |
| P4 | `OUTLINE_PX`, a ball's sides | How finely a ball is cut | Sets how smooth a ball is, not whether anything shows. | — | — | Fine |
| P5 | Sticks numbers' least height (`jointNumbers` shader) | Hides | Already counted in the picture's own pixels. | — | — | Fine |

The 2D picture path (SVG, EMF) has no threshold that hides anything. The `least` values in `lib/figure` belong to reading PDFs.

**The rule that follows:** a threshold that *hides* must be counted in the picture's own pixels; a *floor* may be counted in the canvas's.

- **R7, R8 (a rule, now changed).** The pictures kept to a rule. `docs/EDITOR-2D.md`, *Charges, R and S put by hand* (the maintainer, 2026-10-10), said "a charge so put is in an exported picture (R and S never are)", and a molecule in 3D kept to the same, so they differed from the canvas by a decision, not a mistake. On 2026-10-21 the maintainer reversed it: R/S and E/Z labels go into every picture exactly as the canvas shows them, in 2D and 3D. That means the same labels, places, sizes and fades; a label faded out at that zoom is not in the picture either. The fix comes in its own PR, with the canvas as the known answer.
- **R9–R11 (not yet measured).** There was no fixture: ribbons need a protein, η a complex in 3D, and lines on balls an edited style (no built-in style draws them). Each is drawn by the picture renderer by its code (`ribbonMats`, `withCentres`, `ballLined`), but that is reading, not measuring. Invented fixtures are to be made for them.

Molecules used: an invented amide (30 atoms, numbered 1 to 30), the cholesterol sample in 3D (`src/samples/cholesterol.pdb`), and an invented `C/C=C/[C@@H](O)CC` made 3D by *3D structures*.
